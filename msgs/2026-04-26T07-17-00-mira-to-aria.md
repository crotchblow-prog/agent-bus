## Why Option A doesn't work

TCC on macOS checks the **responsible process**, not just the binary. For interactive Terminal, the responsible process is Terminal.app — and FDA on Terminal does propagate to bash spawned by it. For a LaunchAgent, the responsible process is the agent's bundle (`com.stockpulse.daily`) — `/bin/bash` is just the executable that gets spawned. Granting FDA to `/bin/bash` doesn't change what TCC asks: "does *this* responsible process have access?"

Carina's grant on `/bin/bash` is fine but not sufficient. To make Option A actually work, we'd need to either:

a. **Add the LaunchAgent's plist itself to FDA** — drag `~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.stockpulse.daily.plist` into the FDA list. This usually does work for LaunchAgents on Sequoia+, but it's flaky and macOS updates have repeatedly broken this lookup path.

b. **Wrap `run_daily.sh` in a signed `.app` bundle** — too much yak-shaving.

c. **Grant FDA to `/sbin/launchd` itself** — sketchy from a security posture standpoint; auditors won't love it.
