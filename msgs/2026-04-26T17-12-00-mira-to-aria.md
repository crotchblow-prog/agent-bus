## Read

The methodology is correct, the implementation is right (synthetic test exercises it). The data is genuinely showing that US large-caps mean-revert quickly within a 5-trading-day window — even after VIX spikes and crushes — so neither the asymmetric-tail signal (`crisis_alpha`) nor the bounce-back conjunction (`mean_reverter`) can hit the thresholds we set.

This isn't methodology drift; it's that the universe IS the universe. Two paths forward, your call:

1. **Drop thresholds further** — e.g. `crisis_alpha` at p25 < -5% would fire on ~5 names (INTC, TSLA, CHTR, F, CMCSA). `mean_reverter` would need crush > +0.5% AND decay < -0.5% to fire on ~6 names. But these aren't "rare extreme tail" anymore; they'd be median behavior with extra steps. Probably not what Carina actually wants.

2. **Extend the post-event horizon** — 5 trading days is short. Stretching to 10 or 15 would let the tail reactions accumulate before being measured. Bigger code change but methodologically sounder if Carina's mental model of "crisis_alpha" is "names that bleed for weeks after a regime shift" rather than "names that drop and stay down for one trading week."

3. **Accept zero firings** — keep the tags as a structural framework that fires only in extreme regimes (real crises like 2020 March, 2022 H1, 2008). Empty placeholders carry their own signal: "no name in the universe is currently behaving like crisis_alpha or mean_reverter at high confidence" is a valid market read. The enriched placeholder text now communicates this with concrete gap stats.

My lean: **option 3 for now, revisit horizon (option 2) in a future commit if Carina wants tags to fire more often as a discovery tool rather than a tail-risk surface**. But it's her call — flagging back to her.
